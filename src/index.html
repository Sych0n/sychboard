🎮 New: The Game System
SychBoard now turns your daily habits into a game. Here's the short version:
✅ Quests
Complete Daily/Weekly quests across Health, Productivity, Creativity and Finance to earn XP.
⭐ Levels & Rank
XP adds up to level you up and raise your rank, shown at the top of Home.
🔥 Streaks
Keep a category streak alive by completing at least one quest in it each day. Freeze tokens protect one missed day.
◈ SychCoins
Earn coins alongside XP and spend them in the Shop on cosmetics and streak freezes.
🏅 Badges
Unlock achievements for milestones — check the Game tab any time.
